OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS ×

OBJECTIVE

This player is a hybrid tribute to the golden age of Hi-Fi audio, specifically modeled after the legendary Technics RS-M250 Cassette Deck. It merges vintage aesthetics with modern Web Audio API technology to provide a high-fidelity visual and auditory experience right in your browser.

FEATURES & CONTROLS

  • Transport Controls: Play, Pause, Stop, Fast Forward (Next Track), Rewind (Prev Track), and Precision Seek (+/- 5 seconds).
  • Cassette Mechanism: Realistic tape reels that spin, stop, and change speed based on transport state. The tape supply grows and shrinks in real-time as the song plays.
  • 3D Jewel Case: Displays Album Art on the front and a dynamically generated Tracklist on the back. Click the case to flip it!
  • Playlist Management: Click "EJECT" to load a local folder of music. Supported formats: MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A.
  • Loop & Shuffle: Toggle "LOOP" for Repeat All/One modes. Toggle "MIX" for random shuffle playback.
  • Tape Selectors: Cosmetic switches (Norm/CrO2/Metal) that change the cassette label design instantly.

VISUALIZERS & AUDIO

  • VU Meters: Dual-channel analog needles with warm backlight that bounces to the beat.
  • LED Power Meters: Classic horizontal LED bars showing stereo output levels. (Click area to Toggle ON/OFF).
  • Spectrum Analyzer: A 25-band Real-Time Analyzer (RTA) calibrated from 25Hz to 16kHz with "Peak Hold" falling tiles. (Click area to Toggle ON/OFF).
  • Tone Control: Fully functional Bass and Treble knobs to shape your sound, plus a Master Volume knob.
